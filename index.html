Interview · A2

Who runs
the lab?

Q. Who's behind The Blue Lab?

Hi! I'm Em, the one who runs the lab. I'm a Computer Engineering student with a growing focus on UI design and front-end development, combining my technical background with my lifelong love for art.

Q. How did it start?

Ever since I was young, I've had a passion for visual storytelling, and that curiosity led me to explore the world of graphic design — and later, the web.

“My goal is simple: to create designs that make people smile.”

Q. How do you work?

With intention, personality, and purpose. Whether it's a content template, a branding kit, digital artwork or a website, every piece starts with what you want people to feel.

Q. What's next?

The lab is a reflection of my creative journey. I'm always learning, exploring and growing, and I'm excited to share that with you. Thanks for stopping by — let's create something beautiful together.

Terms & Conditions

The fine print, printed large.
  1. 01

    Rates

    Graphic design services start at ₱250, depending on the content, size, and complexity of the project. Web services are quoted individually. Final pricing will always be confirmed before work begins.

  2. 02

    Payment

    A 50% non-refundable downpayment is required to secure your slot and begin the project. The remaining balance must be settled before final files are released. Payments may be made via GCash, Landbank, or other agreed payment methods and platforms.

  3. 03

    Revisions

    Each project includes up to 3 free revisions. Additional revisions beyond the 3 free revisions are subject to a ₱100 per-edit fee. Major changes or complete redesigns may require additional fees.

  4. 04

    Rush fee

    A ₱150 rush fee applies to urgent projects requiring priority handling. Rush requests are subject to availability and must be arranged in advance.

  5. 05

    Timeline

    Project timelines depend on the scope and complexity of the work. Updates will be provided throughout the process. Delays in client feedback, materials, or payments may affect the delivery schedule.

  6. 06

    Communication & file delivery

    Once your commission is confirmed, all updates, drafts, and revision requests are handled through Telegram (@thebluelab_ph). Final files are sent through a Google Drive link.

  7. 07

    Cancellations

    If the client cancels after work has started, the downpayment will be retained. If the designer is unable to complete the project, a full refund will be issued for payments made.

  8. 08

    Final output

    Final graphic design files are delivered in JPG, PNG, or PDF format through Google Drive, depending on the project. Editable source files are not included unless agreed upon separately. Website deliverables will be discussed before the project begins.

  9. 09

    Usage & ownership

    Designs may be used for the personal or commercial purposes agreed upon. Reselling, redistributing, or modifying designs without permission is not allowed. Original working files and pre-existing design resources remain the designer's property unless otherwise agreed. Completed projects may be featured in the designer's portfolio unless confidentiality is requested.

  10. 10

    Client responsibilities

    Clients must provide accurate content, images, and other necessary materials. Please review all drafts carefully, especially names, dates, spelling, and other details, before approving the final output.

  11. 11

    Agreement

    By submitting a commission request and paying the required downpayment, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms & Conditions.
